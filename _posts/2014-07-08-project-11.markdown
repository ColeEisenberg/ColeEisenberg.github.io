description: First, a quick testimet to the importance of networking. An unassuming conversation in a waiting room led to me being hired as a construction worker, which ultimately opened the door to my first engineering design project. <br>  
 One day at work, the foreman and I were tasked with mounting three industrial cage lights to the underside of an off-pitch piece of corrugated metal. Traditional construction methods proved insufficient for this unique challenge, but I saw it as an opportunity to apply my skills in 3D printing and CAD.
client-link: https://coleeisenberg.github.io/
img1: CorrugatedProfile.png
paragraph1: Simple trigonometry and the standard dimensions of corrugated metal provided a solid starting point for designing the mounting surface. A few iterations with test pieces helped refine the profile and angle to perfection. <br>
 Since the piece was intended to remain outdoors indefinitely, I chose to print it using acrylonitrile styrene acrylate (ASA) for its weather-resistant properties. An electrical box was to be nestled inside, meaning the two dissimilar materials would expand and contract at different rates. By using the thermal expansion coefficients I determined the required tolerances, and was able to ensure the design would hold up over environmental stresses.
