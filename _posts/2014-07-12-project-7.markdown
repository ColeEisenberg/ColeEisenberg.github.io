paragraph3: The Corvette was an awesome and reliable car, but I wanted an additional car to get slightly better MPG. This led me to purchase a '99 Toyota Camry (if you haven't noticed, I like old cars). Thanks to Toyota's Kaizen manufacturing process and some preventative maintenance, I trusted this car on a road trip from New York to the West Coast and back. No hiccups other than some flat tires in Montana.
img4: CamryHeadGasket.png

paragraph4: While I do love old Toyotas, after 200,000 miles, some components won't handle the years fatigue resulting from less-than-conservative driving. Back in September 2024, I started to notice the symptoms of head gasket failure. Trusting my mechanical abilities, I decided to tackle the project on my own. The project lasted somewhere between a couple and a few nights, and I took enough re-assembly notes to write a thesis. I emerged with a once again functional Camry and an enhanced understanding of the principles that *drive* internal combustion engines.
